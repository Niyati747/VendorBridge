# 🚀 VendorBridge

### Smart Vendor Management & Procurement Platform

VendorBridge is a modern procurement solution built for the Odoo Hackathon that simplifies vendor collaboration, quotation management, approvals, and procurement tracking through a unified dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Businesses often manage procurement through emails, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems, leading to:

- Delayed approvals
- Vendor communication gaps
- Difficult quotation comparisons
- Limited procurement visibility

---

## 💡 Solution

VendorBridge provides a centralized platform to:

✅ Manage Vendors  
✅ Create & Track RFQs  
✅ Collect Quotations  
✅ Compare Vendor Bids  
✅ Approve Procurement Requests  
✅ Manage Purchase Orders & Invoices  
✅ Generate Reports & Insights

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏢 Vendor Management
Centralized vendor profiles and tracking.

### 📄 RFQ & Quotations
Create RFQs, receive quotations, and monitor responses.

### ⚖️ Quotation Comparison
Compare vendor proposals side-by-side for smarter decisions.

### ✅ Approval Workflow
Streamlined approval process with status tracking.

### 🛒 Purchase Orders & Invoices
End-to-end procurement lifecycle management.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
Procurement insights and performance reporting.
